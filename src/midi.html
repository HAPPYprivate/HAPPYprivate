Happy Macro

A macro tool that automatically plays pieces on the piano of compatible games, reading the .happy format made with Sheet Studio.

Happy Macro is not a MIDI editor. It reads .happy data and plays it. Games: Roblox, Sky, and others once support is confirmed.

FREE

Generate

Turn video or audio into a starting sketch. Outputs: MIDI, a draft .happy, and .mgp for Macro Gamer.

Not a perfect converter. Results depend on source quality, audio clarity, source choice, detection settings and manual editing. Finish the sketch in Sheet Studio before using it as a complete .happy file.

.mgp is for Macro Gamer, an independent program by another developer. It is not part of HAPPY Midi and is not an official partnership.

Paid

Check Display for MIDI

Analyses what is on screen to detect notes from piano images or video, and exports the result as a MIDI file. Several detection methods and settings are available.

Accuracy depends on video quality, key and note clarity, image motion, and the detection method and settings you choose. It aims to help extract MIDI from visual piano content; it is not 100% accurate on every video.
